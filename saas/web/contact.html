What to include

A little context gets you a faster, more useful answer:

  • Product or strategy question: the strategy name and coin, or a screenshot of what you're seeing.
  • Billing: the email on your account (never your card details).
  • Security issue: what you found and how to reproduce it. We take these seriously and will respond directly, not with a form letter.
  • Press or partnerships: a line on who you are and what you're asking about.

zengtrade is a small team, and every message is read by a person. We don't outsource support to a ticket queue.

About zengtrade

zengtrade is a crypto algo studio: systematic, regime-aware trading strategies that run on live market data, 24/7. Every strategy is paper-first: you watch it trade on real prices with fake money until the evidence says otherwise, before a dollar is ever at risk.

It's non-custodial. When you do go live, execution runs on your own exchange account with your own API keys. zengtrade never holds your funds and never takes a cut of your trades.

The product is built around one idea: survival first, honesty always. That means no performance figures unless they're sourced and verified net of real costs, no promises about returns, and no pretending a backtest is a guarantee. Trading crypto carries real risk of loss, including all of your capital. See the Risk Disclaimer.

zengtrade is software and educational tooling. It is not a broker, exchange, custodian, or investment adviser, and it isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any exchange or broker. See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the specifics.
