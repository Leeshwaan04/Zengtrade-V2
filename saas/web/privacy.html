Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
⚠️ Draft template, review with a lawyer for GDPR / India DPDP compliance before launch.

What we collect

Account data (email), authentication data (handled by our auth provider), your strategy deployments and simulated trade history, subscription/billing status (handled by our payment provider), and basic usage/analytics data.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your card details (handled by the payment provider) and, in the current paper-only product, we do not collect exchange API keys. If live features are enabled, any keys you provide are encrypted and stored solely to operate your chosen strategies.

How we use it

To run the service, operate your paper book, process subscriptions, communicate service messages, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal data.

Processors

We use third-party processors including our authentication/database provider (Supabase), payment provider (merchant of record), and email provider. Each processes data only to provide their service.

Your rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your data by contacting hello@zengtrade.in. We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

Security

Data is isolated per user at the database level (row-level security), encrypted in transit, and any sensitive credentials are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for your own account security.

Contact

Questions: hello@zengtrade.in.
