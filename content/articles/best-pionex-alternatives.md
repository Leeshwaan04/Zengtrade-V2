## The Problem with Exchange-Locked Trading Bots

When an exchange provides its own proprietary bots, several structural conflicts arise:

1. **Custodial Counterparty Risk:** Depositing funds into an intermediary exchange introduces insolvency and regulatory freeze risks.
2. **Hidden Execution Spreads:** Headline fees may appear low (e.g. 0.05%), but order book depth, internal routing, and wide spreads on illiquid pairs often cost significantly more than the stated fee.
3. **Loss of Tier Privileges:** Advanced traders with VIP fee tiers or BNB fee deductions on Binance lose those cost benefits when forced onto secondary platforms.
