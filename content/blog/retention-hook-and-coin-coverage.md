This is the first post on the zengtrade blog, so a quick note on what this section is for: short,
dated, honest updates on what actually shipped, not a highlight reel. If a post here says something
shipped, you can go check it. If it didn't happen yet, we'll say so.

## A notification for your first closed trade

Until this week, deploying a paper strategy and having its first trade close produced no signal
anywhere in the product unless you happened to open the Forward Test tab yourself. That's now
fixed: the first time a paper trade on your account closes, you get a notification with the real
result, win or lose, and a link to the full evidence.

It only fires once, for the first trade, not every trade after. A strategy can close several
trades a day, and a popup for each one would turn into noise instead of a signal.

## Growing real coin coverage

The coin strategy library started at 150 coins, ranked by real market cap and filtered to whatever
is actually tradable on Binance spot. That's expanding in stages toward the real ceiling: every
coin on Binance USDT spot that isn't a stablecoin or a tokenized non-crypto asset, which puts the
real number somewhere around 488 coins today. This isn't about hitting a round page-count target,
it's about covering more of what's actually real and tradable. Padding with duplicate or thin
pages isn't something we're willing to trade for a bigger number.

## What's next

Wider coin coverage continues in stages, and a dedicated Content Strategist role now owns finding
real, non-duplicate content worth adding as the product grows, not just more combinatorial pages.
